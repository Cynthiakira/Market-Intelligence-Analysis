# Market-Intelligence-Analysis
This market intelligence study highlights notable differences in land values across the selected towns within the Alimosho/Ipaja axis of Lagos. Aboru emerges as the highest-value market at approximately ₦206,000/sqm, followed by Agbelekale at ₦171,000/sqm, indicating stronger market demand and development activity in these locations. Ajasa/Amikanle occupies a mid-market position, while Oke-Odo and Oki represent the more affordable segments of the market.
The spatial analysis further reveals a clear concentration of higher land values around Aboru and Agbelekale, with values gradually declining toward Oke-Odo and Oki. These findings provide valuable insight for property valuation, investment decision-making, land acquisition, and urban development planning. Overall, the study establishes a reliable market benchmark and demonstrates how geographic visualization and market data can be combined to generate actionable land market intelligence
